VideoGrabber

A fast browser with a built-in downloader. Open any video, tap download, pick the quality: up to 4K and 8K.

Download

Pick your device. Everything the app needs is included, so there's nothing else to install.

macOS

Same app as on Windows, with tabs and the side panel. macOS 11 or later.

Apple Silicon M1–M4 · .dmg Intel Mac .dmg
How to install
  1. Open the .dmg and drag VideoGrabber into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon.

Windows

Installer for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Adds Start-menu and desktop shortcuts.

Windows installer 64-bit · .exe
How to install
  1. Run VideoGrabber-Setup-win-x64.exe.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. This appears because the app isn't code-signed yet.
  3. Follow the installer. You can choose the install folder.

Android

Mobile app built for phones, with tabs and a floating download button on every page. Android 10 or later.

Most phones arm64 · .apk Universal any device · .apk
How to install
  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
  2. Allow your browser to install unknown apps when Android asks.
  3. Tip: in YouTube, Instagram or any app, tap Share → VideoGrabber to download that video.
  4. Older phone? Use the armv7 APK.

iPhone & iPad

The iOS app is in development. Apple doesn't allow downloader apps on the App Store, so it will install through AltStore or Sideloadly.

What it does

Browse normally. When a page has a video, VideoGrabber shows every quality it comes in.

Every quality8K, 4K, 1440p, 1080p60, HDR or audio only (MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC), with the file size shown before you download.
1000+ sitesYouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, Vimeo, Reddit, Dailymotion and many more.
TabsOpen several tabs. Each one detects its own video and keeps its own list.
Works when logged inSign in inside the app to download videos that need your account.
Download historyEvery download is saved in a list, detail or card view, with search and filters.
Light & darkFollows your system theme, or pick one in Settings.
